{% trans "5. How employee account and invoice data is handled" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Employee registration and profile information is used only for internal business administration, account access, invoice preparation, communication, and related operational purposes. Employee data is accessible only to authorized personnel who need it for legitimate business reasons. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Address and billing information provided by employees may be used to pre-fill Simple Invoice forms and to generate invoice documents required for payment and internal company processing. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} When a Simple Invoice is submitted, an invoice record is stored in the application database and a PDF invoice is generated from the submitted information. The generated PDF may be sent to the employee email address and the company email address and is also stored in private cloud storage for authorized access during the applicable retention period. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Stored invoice PDF files are not intended to be publicly accessible. Access to an invoice through the website is restricted to authenticated users who are authorized to access the corresponding invoice. Temporary, time-limited access may be created when an authorized employee requests to download a stored invoice. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Temporary files created on the application server during invoice generation, including temporary spreadsheet and PDF files, are used only for processing the invoice. These temporary files are removed after the invoice processing task is completed and are not used as permanent invoice storage. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Previous Simple Invoice records are retained so that employees may access their invoice history during the applicable retention period. New invoice submissions do not automatically replace or delete previously stored invoice records that remain within that retention period. {% endblocktrans %}
